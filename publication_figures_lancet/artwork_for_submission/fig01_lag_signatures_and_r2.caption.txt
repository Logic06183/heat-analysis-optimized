Figure 1: Multi-system temperature lag-response signatures and model adequacy. (a) Heatmap of mean absolute SHAP values for temperature exposure at seven lag windows, six single days (0, 1, 3, 7, 14, 21 days) and a 30-day cumulative mean, shown for the 10 retained biomarkers and grouped by physiological domain; colour encodes physiological domain and shading within each row encodes row-normalised SHAP magnitude. The outlined cell in each row marks that biomarker's peak lag, and † marks the 30-day cumulative mean, which is not a single-day lag. (b) Cross-validated R-squared by biomarker, from 5-fold cross-validation grouped by participant, with 50-replicate bootstrap 95% confidence intervals; the dashed line marks the R-squared 0·05 retention threshold. Because every value is measured in participants the model was not trained on, the intervals show how reliably each small signal reproduces. For body fat percentage and waist-hip ratio, measured once for most participants, R-squared marks standing differences between people, not the magnitude of a heat response.

10 of 24 biomarkers met the R² ≥ 0·05 retention criterion.
